What 40% more load does when generators run out of reactive power

Scale every load in the IEEE 14-bus case to 140% and solve it twice: once letting generators produce whatever reactive power it takes to hold their voltage set-points, and once holding them to their real Qmin–Qmax. The second solve is checked against pandapower, an independent open-source solver, run on the same case file.

Limits. Balanced steady state only: no dynamics, faults or protection. Line ratings are illustrative (1.5× the base-case flow, at least 50 MVA) because the public case ships without them. Generator reactive limits are enforced except at the slack bus, which has to balance the system; its Q is shown against its limits but never pinned. Educational, not a planning tool.
